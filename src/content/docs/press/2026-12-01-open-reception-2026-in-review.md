2026-12-01

After our version 1 release in May 2026, we have rolled out many additional features in the past months. The new practice newsletter keeps doctors informed about the latest developments and updates in OpenReception. A patient PDF helps patients to discuss data protection and secure appointment scheduling with their doctors.

## New Features

The most exciting new features of the past months are:

- [Regular Absences](/agents/add-absence/): Allows individual actors to be regularly absent without having to enter this manually. Particularly relevant for practices with multiple employees and part-time employment.
- [Deadlines](/channels/edit-channel/#deadlines) in which appointments can be booked and canceled.
- [Appointment Information](/channels/edit-channel/#appointment-information) displayed to patients after booking to prepare for the appointment.
- Many new calendar features such as [grouped view by actor](/calendar/#grouping), [reschedule appointment](/calendar/move-appointment/), [book follow-up appointment](/calendar/follow-up-appointment/) and [waiting time and waiting room management](/calendar/appointment-progress/).
- [Notifications about canceled appointments](/staff/notifications/#notification-types) include the contact details of the person who canceled.
- Store up to three [Passkeys per account](/account/manage-passkeys/): This allows practices to securely store a hardware passkey in the safe for additional security.
- Support for Windows Hello (more on this below)

An overview of the various features can be found in the section [Plus, Premium & Management Features](/getting-started/#plus-premium--management-features).

## »Hello, Windows Hello«

OpenReception has supported Windows Hello since the summer. This removes one of the biggest obstacles to adoption in practices.

Windows PRF passkeys are supported on current Windows 11 installations and enable secure and convenient login. Logging in with a fingerprint or facial recognition is particularly easy.

With the integration into Windows Hello, we consider the login issue in OpenReception solved, as by now all practices should have switched to Windows 11.

On macOS and Linux, biometric passkeys are also supported, making secure and convenient login possible here as well.

We recommend that all practices, in addition to using quick login via Windows Hello or similar, also use hardware passkeys for additional security.

Further information can be found in our section on [Passkeys](/getting-started/#passkeys).

## PDF for Patients Who Want Data Protection

In response to numerous requests from the community, we have created a PDF for patients that helps them discuss data protection and secure appointment scheduling with their doctors.

We have developed two versions:

- A letter version where patients can fill out and expand a letter like a form.
- An informational version that informs doctors about the benefits and possibilities of OpenReception.

Both versions can be found in the section [Patient PDF](/community/patient-pdf).

## Funded OpenSource Software

OpenReception is free OpenSource software under an AGPL v3 license. This means anyone can run OpenReception for themselves and others. Everyone can contribute.

The development of the OpenReception prototype was funded until 31.03.2026 by the Federal Ministry for Research, Technology and Space. With this, the BMFTR underscores the necessity of an open, free, and secure appointment solution for medical practices.
